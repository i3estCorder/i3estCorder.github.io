HIGH SCORE: 0
TIME 0 LENGTH 3 LEVEL 1 ENEMY 1

Arrow keys / WASD to move. Eat pixels, avoid the red enemies.

HOW TO PLAY

Stay in the loop.

  • Collect the yellow pixels to grow.
  • Score = survival time + snake length.
  • Every 30 seconds, one more Enemy appears.
  • Hit a wall, yourself, or an Enemy: game over.
